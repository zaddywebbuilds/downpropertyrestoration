Questions

What people ask before they view a room.

Is the room actually furnished?
Yes. It comes with a bed frame and mattress, a desk and chair, blinds on the window, an overhead light and a smoke alarm, plus fridge space of your own in the common area. You do not need to buy or move furniture in.
What do I share with the other people in the house?
The kitchen, the two sitting rooms, the dining area, the shower room and the toilet. The shower and the toilet are separate rooms, so two people can get ready at the same time. Your own room has a door that closes.
Can I see the room before I commit to anything?
Yes, and you should. Call or text to arrange a time, walk the house, and look at the kitchen and bathrooms for yourself before you decide anything.
How quickly could I move in?
As soon as the paperwork is done. Because the room is already furnished, there is nothing to deliver or assemble first.
What does the rent include, and is there parking?
Ask when you call. Rates, what is covered for utilities, internet and laundry, and parking are all best confirmed directly, since they can differ from room to room.
